Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

auto-uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

With the account owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uploads video files and sets video metadata (title, description, tags, visibility) on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store personal data of any other user.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only in the owner's private environment and is used only to upload the owner's videos. No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through this project's GitHub repository.
